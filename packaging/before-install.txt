Valdo Sniper - before you install

You need:
  - Windows 10 or 11 (not a virtual machine)
  - Google Chrome (installed normally)
  - Path of Exile

For the two-client setup:
  - Account A = buyer: holds the currency, does every purchase.
  - Account B = the account you play on.

After installing, Valdo Sniper opens. Click "Open the sniper Chrome"
in the overlay and follow the setup page it shows:
  1. Install Tampermonkey and turn on "Allow User Scripts".
  2. Install the browser script.
  3. Log in to pathofexile.com as account A only.
Then paste your Better Trading export into the import box and click
"Save & open tabs". The live tabs open by themselves, paced so the
trade site's search limit is never hit.

Every session: park client A in your hideout, click into it and press
Ctrl+Alt+B to mark it as the buyer. Play on client B. On an alert,
press the backtick key (`). You buy every item by hand.

Nothing types or clicks inside either game client. One key press
makes one click on the trade site.
